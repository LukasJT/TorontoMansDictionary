A few sample questions

In case the interactive version above doesn't load for you (or you'd rather just read), here are five real questions from the pool, static, no JavaScript required:

What does "peak" mean in Toronto slang?

Something unfortunate, unfair, or genuinely bad — "that's peak" is roughly equivalent to "that sucks." Full entry: Peak.

What does "wallahi" mean?

"I swear to God" in Arabic — used constantly across the GTA thanks to the city's large Somali and Arab communities. Full entry: Wallahi.

What does "bare" mean in Toronto slang?

"A lot" or "very" — the opposite of its standard English meaning. Full entry: Bare.

What does "gwap" mean?

Money. Full entry: Gwap / Gwop.

Why is Toronto called "the six"?

It refers to the 1998 amalgamation of six municipalities into one city, plus a nod to the 416 area code. Full entry: The Six / The 6ix.
