About this dictionary

Toronto slang isn't one thing. It's Jamaican Patois out of Jane and Finch, Somali and Arabic from the city's East African and Middle Eastern communities, hockey-rink trash talk, TTC nicknames, and a bit of Drake — all layered on top of each other by one of the most multicultural cities on earth. Read the full history for how that actually happened.

This site collects real, researched entries on that vocabulary: where a word likely comes from, what it actually means, and how it's used — not a guess, and not an inside joke invented for this page. Full source list is in SOURCES.md.

Slang moves fast and origins are debated even among linguists — treat this as a living reference, not a courtroom transcript. See something wrong or missing? and it'll get fixed.
