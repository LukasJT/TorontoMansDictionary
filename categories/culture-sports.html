Toronto Culture & Sports Slang

Words tied to Toronto's culture at large rather than to a specific immigrant-language root — sports (the Raptors' "We The North"), music (Drake's OVO), and food (patty, roti) that have become part of the city's shared identity.

This one's tied to Toronto's culture at large — sports, food or music that's become part of the city's identity.

6 words in this category

  • Blem — High, particularly from smoking marijuana.
  • Flex — As a verb, to show off or stunt. Tacked onto a noun, it can also mean you're about to go do something ("gym flex" = heading to the gym).
  • OVO — "October's Very Own" — Drake's Toronto-based record label and clothing brand, named for his October birthday. Shorthand for the whole OVO/Drake cultural footprint on the city.
  • Patty — The Jamaican beef (or chicken, or veggie) patty — a flaky, curry-coloured pastry sold at corner stores and bakeries across the city. Not slang exactly, but non-negotiable Toronto cultural vocabulary.
  • Roti — A Caribbean flatbread wrapped around curried meat, chickpeas or vegetables — a GTA staple order, especially in Scarborough.
  • We The North — The Toronto Raptors' rallying slogan, unveiled in April 2014 ahead of the playoffs. It grew into a citywide identity — Canada's only NBA team representing the whole country, not just the city.
