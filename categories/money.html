Toronto Money Slang

Money slang moves fast through hip-hop and drill culture generally, and Toronto's rap scene has adopted (and sometimes localized) a well-worn set of terms for cash, in amounts small and large.

Money slang travels fast through hip-hop and drill culture, and the GTA scene has its own well-worn set of terms for it.

4 words in this category

  • Bag / Secure the Bag — Money, or a financial opportunity. "Secure the bag" means locking down something profitable.
  • Bands — Large amounts of money, typically counted in thousands (a "band" = $1,000, named after the paper band around a stack of bills).
  • Gwap / Gwop — Money.
  • Racks — Money, usually in reference to a substantial amount ($1,000 or a stack of bills).
