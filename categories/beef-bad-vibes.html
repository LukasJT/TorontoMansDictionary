Toronto Beef & Bad Vibes Slang

The flip side of hype vocabulary: words for when something is bad, someone's annoying you, or there's tension. Tone does a lot of the work here — the same word said flat versus shouted can mean mild annoyance or real anger.

This is the vocabulary for when something, or someone, isn't hitting right.

Advertisement

10 words in this category

  • Cheesed / Cheesing — Angry, annoyed, or deeply irritated. To say someone is "cheesing" you means they're making you angry — the intensity ranges from mild annoyance said flatly to real anger when it's shouted.
  • Chirping / Chirp — Trash talk — an insult or dig thrown at someone, especially rooted in Canadian hockey culture where players "chirp" opponents. In everyday Toronto speech it just means friendly (or not-so-friendly) roasting.
  • Dry — Blunt, unenthusiastic or straight-up boring — describing a person's response or a whole situation with no energy to it.
  • Mix Up — Being wishy-washy, flaky or sus (suspicious) — someone who can't be trusted to be straight with you.
  • Nize — To be quiet or stop talking — an imperative, usually heard as "nize that" or "nize your beaks" (beaks meaning mouths).
  • Opp Block — The block or area belonging to your opps — enemy territory.
  • Opps — Short for "opposition" — enemies, rivals, people you're beefing with.
  • Peak — Something unfortunate, unfair or genuinely bad. "That's peak" means "that sucks."
  • Wasteman / Waste Yute — Someone lazy, unmotivated or generally useless — a "waste yute" is the younger version of the same insult.
  • Weak — Lame, disappointing, not up to standard — a step below "peak" but the same general idea.
