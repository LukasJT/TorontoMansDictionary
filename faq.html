What does "mans" mean in Toronto slang?

"Mans" is used as a stand-in for "I" or "me," e.g. "mans is tired." It's believed to come from Jamaican Patois as spoken in Toronto's Caribbean communities — see the full dictionary entry and Derek Denis's academic paper on the usage, cited in our history page.

Why is Toronto called "the six" or "the 6ix"?

It refers to the 1998 amalgamation of six municipalities — old Toronto, North York, Etobicoke, Scarborough, York and East York — into one city, plus a nod to the 416 area code. Toronto rapper Jimmy Prime used it in street culture before Drake globalized it with Views From The 6. Full entry: The Six / The 6ix.

What is Multicultural Toronto English (MTE)?

MTE is the academic name for what's commonly called "Toronto slang" — a multiethnolect that developed among young people in Toronto's multicultural inner suburbs. It's named and actively researched by linguist Derek Denis at the University of Toronto Mississauga. Read the full breakdown on our history page.

Where did Toronto slang actually come from?

Mainly Jane and Finch, Rexdale, and parts of Scarborough, through two big immigration waves: Jamaican immigration from the 1960s-1980s, and Somali immigration after Somalia's civil war in the early 1990s. See the full history for the details and sources.

Is using Toronto slang cultural appropriation?

It's genuinely debated, and worth taking seriously rather than waving away. The communities who built this vocabulary have described real discomfort watching words that once marked them as outsiders get flattened and monetized by people with no connection to where they came from — see the "From the block to global" section of our history page for the fuller picture, including how we try to handle it on this site by crediting origins rather than treating words as ownerless internet slang.

What does "bare" mean in Toronto slang?

"A lot" or "very" — the opposite of its standard English meaning. "There's bare people at the mall" means there are a lot of people there. Full entry: Bare.

What does "peak" mean in Toronto slang?

Something unfortunate, unfair, or genuinely bad — "that's peak" is roughly "that sucks." Full entry: Peak.

How is this different from Urban Dictionary?

Urban Dictionary is open, crowd-submitted and largely unmoderated. This is a curated, researched reference focused specifically on Toronto/GTA slang — every entry is compiled from published sources rather than anonymous submissions, with a full source list in SOURCES.md. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Urban Dictionary in any way.

Can I submit a Toronto slang word that's missing?

Yes — use the button on any page. It opens a short form and hands off a pre-filled email to the site owner with your suggested term, definition and example.
