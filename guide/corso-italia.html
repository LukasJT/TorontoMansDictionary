Corso Italia

As College Street's Little Italy grew more commercial, Italian Toronto shifted north to St. Clair Avenue West — a strip that got its name, and its identity, cemented by one unforgettable night in 1982.

Advertisement

Corso Italia is a commercial strip along St. Clair Avenue West, Toronto's second major Italian enclave after the older Little Italy on College Street. Italian families began opening butcher shops, fruit markets and cafés along St. Clair West in the 1950s and '60s, and the northward shift accelerated in the 1970s as College Street grew more commercial and residential Italian-Toronto life moved toward St. Clair.

The 1982 World Cup

The defining event in the strip's popular memory is Italy's win at the 1982 FIFA World Cup. St. Clair West erupted in celebration, with crowds reported between roughly 250,000 and 300,000 people shutting down close to 20 blocks of the avenue — flags, horns and impromptu parades still referenced as the moment "Corso Italia" cemented itself as a name and identity, distinct from the older College Street enclave. The Corso Italia Business Improvement Area was established in 1983 and continues to run community events today.
