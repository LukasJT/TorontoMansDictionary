A $9.1 billion project that took until 2026 to open

The Eglinton Crosstown LRT

Construction began in 2011 on a line originally slated to open in 2020. It finally opened to riders in February 2026, after costs rose well beyond the original budget.

Advertisement

Construction on the Eglinton Crosstown began in 2011, at an initial estimated cost of about $9.1 billion for a design-build-finance-maintain contract with the Crosslinx Transit Solutions consortium. The line was originally slated to open in 2020, but the date slipped repeatedly through the 2020s; documents obtained by CBC Toronto in 2022 showed project costs had risen to at least $12.8 billion.

A 2026 opening

The line achieved "substantial completion" on December 5, 2025, following a Revenue Service Demonstration period, at which point full operational control transferred to the TTC. Line 5 Eglinton opened to TTC customers on February 8, 2026. The initial phase runs approximately 19 km from Kennedy Station to Mount Dennis, with 25 stops mixing underground stations and surface-level stops along Eglinton Avenue.
