The Toronto Sceptres

The Toronto Sceptres began play in the Professional Women's Hockey League's inaugural 2023-24 season, after decades of fragile, short-lived leagues failed to give women's hockey a stable pro home.

Advertisement

The Toronto Sceptres began play in the 2023-24 season as one of six founding teams of the Professional Women's Hockey League, a new North American league formed after a merger between two competing organizations, the Premier Hockey Federation and the Professional Women's Hockey Players' Association, which had spent years pushing for a single, financially stable league rather than fragmented, short-lived competitors. Toronto's franchise played its first home games at the Mattamy Athletic Centre, the former Maple Leaf Gardens, drawing large crowds for a debut women's hockey season.

Decades of false starts

Women's professional hockey in North America had gone through several earlier attempts that folded or struggled for stable funding, including the National Women's Hockey League and its eventual rebrand as the Premier Hockey Federation. The PWHL's launch, backed by significant private investment and built around six well-resourced markets including Toronto, was widely covered as the first genuinely stable attempt to establish women's hockey as an ongoing professional sport rather than a series of short-lived experiments.
