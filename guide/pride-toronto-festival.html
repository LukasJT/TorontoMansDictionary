Pride Toronto

Our bathhouse raids guide covers the 1981 police raids that sparked Toronto's first Pride march. This is the story of what that single march grew into: a multi-day festival now drawing millions of people every June.

Our bathhouse raids guide covers the 1981 police raids that sparked Toronto's first Pride march — a "Lesbian and Gay Pride Day" that drew an estimated 1,500 participants. The event remained a loosely organized, community-run affair for several years before a coalition of community groups formally established it as an organization by the mid-1980s, eventually incorporating as Pride Toronto.

Millions of attendees

What began as a single march grew into a multi-day festival, typically running about a week and culminating on the last weekend of June, centred on Toronto's Church-Wellesley Village. Attendance figures reported by organizers show dramatic growth: roughly 1.7 million in 2019, and over 3 million by 2024. On the strength of those figures, Pride Toronto is frequently described as the largest Pride festival in North America and among the largest in the world.
