A farm equipment fortune paid for one of Toronto's most storied concert halls

Massey Manufacturing

Hart Massey's farm machinery company grew into Massey-Harris, one of Canada's largest manufacturers — and the family fortune it produced built both Massey Hall and Massey College.

Advertisement

Massey Manufacturing built its business on farm machinery, growing through the 19th century under Hart Massey into one of the largest agricultural equipment makers in Canada, headquartered in Toronto. A merger with rival manufacturer A. Harris, Son and Company in 1891 created Massey-Harris, a company that went on to dominate Canadian farm equipment manufacturing and later, as Massey Ferguson after a further merger, became a genuinely global agricultural machinery brand.

A fortune turned into landmarks

The wealth the company generated funded some of Toronto's most enduring cultural landmarks: Hart Massey built Massey Hall, which opened in 1894 as a gift to the city, and later generations of the family endowed Massey College at the University of Toronto in the 1960s. The Massey name, attached to a concert hall and a graduate college rather than to the manufacturing business itself, is now far better known to most Torontonians than the farm equipment company that paid for both.
