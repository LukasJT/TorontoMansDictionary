An invoicing tool a web designer built because he was sick of Microsoft Word invoices

FreshBooks

Founded in Toronto in 2003 after its creator got fed up manually formatting invoices in Word, FreshBooks grew into one of the most widely used small-business accounting and invoicing platforms in North America.

Advertisement

FreshBooks began in Toronto in 2003, founded by web designer Mike McDerment after he grew frustrated manually reformatting invoices in Microsoft Word for his freelance design clients, a tedious, error-prone process shared by countless other small business owners and freelancers at the time. He built a simple web-based invoicing tool to solve his own problem, then realized the same tool could serve a much larger market of small businesses facing an identical annoyance.

A widely used small-business staple

FreshBooks grew steadily from that simple invoicing tool into a fuller cloud-based accounting platform aimed specifically at freelancers and small businesses, a market segment that larger enterprise accounting software companies had generally underserved. The company remained headquartered in Toronto through its growth into one of the more widely used small-business accounting platforms in North America, part of a wave of Toronto-founded software companies that built national and international customer bases from a Canadian home base.
