The Rivoli

The Rivoli on Queen Street West became a key incubator for Toronto comedy and music in the 1980s, hosting the early stage shows of The Kids in the Hall before the troupe landed their own television series.

Advertisement

The Rivoli opened on Queen Street West in the early 1980s, combining a restaurant, bar and small performance space at a time when that stretch of Queen Street was emerging as a hub for the city's alternative art, music and comedy scenes rather than the more commercial strip it later became. Its back room booked a mix of music, spoken word and sketch comedy, giving developing performers a low-stakes venue to test material in front of live audiences.

A launching pad for The Kids in the Hall

The Rivoli became particularly significant as an early home for the sketch comedy troupe The Kids in the Hall, who performed regular live shows there in the mid-1980s before being discovered and eventually landing their own television series on CBC and later HBO. The venue's role in that troupe's development made it one of the more consequential small stages in Canadian comedy history, and it has continued operating as a music and event venue on Queen Street West in the decades since.
