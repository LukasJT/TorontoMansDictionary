Jane and Finch

The intersection and surrounding neighbourhood in northwest Toronto, shaped heavily by Jamaican, Somali and other Caribbean/African communities. Along with Scarborough and Rexdale, it's one of the corridors most credited with shaping modern Toronto slang.
