Gyaldem

Refers to a group of girls or women collectively. Commonly used to describe a crew of women out together.

Advertisement

How it's used

This is relationship vocabulary — how people in the GTA refer to friends, crews, and showing respect.

“Bare gyaldem pulled up to the party last night.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Jamaican Patois plural ("gyal" + "dem," meaning "them"), the female counterpart to "mandem". It's one of many Toronto terms that trace back to Jamaican Patois, brought into the city through Toronto's Caribbean communities. Read the fuller story in our history of Toronto slang.
