The Dot

Even shorter version of T Dot. Just "the Dot."

Advertisement

How it's used

Words like this are about place — the specific streets, boundaries and nicknames that tell you exactly which part of the GTA someone means.

“He's not from the Dot, he's a 905 kid.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Shortened form of T Dot. Like a lot of GTA slang, its exact origin is debated even among people who use it every day — see our full history of Toronto slang for the bigger picture on how this vocabulary formed.
