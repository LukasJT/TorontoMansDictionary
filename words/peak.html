Peak

Something unfortunate, unfair or genuinely bad. "That's peak" means "that sucks."

Advertisement

How it's used

This is the vocabulary for when something, or someone, isn't hitting right.

“Missed the last train by two minutes — that's peak.”

Where it comes from

Origin: UK-influenced street slang, common in the GTA. It overlaps with UK street slang — multiethnolects like Toronto's Multicultural Toronto English and London's Multicultural London English share real linguistic DNA. More in our history page.
