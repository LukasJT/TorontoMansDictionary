Wagwan

"What's going on?" / "What's up?" — one of the most common Patois-rooted greetings heard across the GTA.

Advertisement

How it's used

This is conversational glue — words and tags that hold a sentence together in everyday GTA speech.

“Wagwan, haven't seen you in mad long.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Jamaican Patois: "what's going on". It's one of many Toronto terms that trace back to Jamaican Patois, brought into the city through Toronto's Caribbean communities. Read the fuller story in our history of Toronto slang.
