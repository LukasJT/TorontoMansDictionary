Miskeen

Describes a person or situation as pathetic, sad, or pitiful. One of the clearest markers of Toronto slang's Somali/Arabic influence, and it got a huge mainstream spotlight when Drake tattooed the word above his eye in 2023.

Advertisement

How it's used

This is the vocabulary for when something, or someone, isn't hitting right.

“He got left on read for a week — that's kind of miskeen.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Arabic word ("miskin," meaning poor/unfortunate) that entered Toronto slang through the city's large Somali community. It's part of the Somali- and Arabic-rooted vocabulary that entered Toronto slang through the city's East African and Middle Eastern communities. Read more in our history of Toronto slang.
