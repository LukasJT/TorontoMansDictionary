Nuff Respect

"Thank you" or "I appreciate that," delivered with real sincerity. "Nuff" here means "enough," as in "plenty."

Advertisement

How it's used

This is relationship vocabulary — how people in the GTA refer to friends, crews, and showing respect.

“You helped me move that whole apartment — nuff respect.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Jamaican Patois. It's one of many Toronto terms that trace back to Jamaican Patois, brought into the city through Toronto's Caribbean communities. Read the fuller story in our history of Toronto slang.
