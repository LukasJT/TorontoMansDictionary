OVO

"October's Very Own" — Drake's Toronto-based record label and clothing brand, named for his October birthday. Shorthand for the whole OVO/Drake cultural footprint on the city.

How it's used

This one's tied to Toronto's culture at large — sports, food or music that's become part of the city's identity.

“He's got the OVO owl on his jacket.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Drake's Toronto-founded label and brand. It's tied to Toronto's music and sports culture more than to a specific immigrant-language root — see our "from the block to global" section for how that side of Toronto slang spread.
