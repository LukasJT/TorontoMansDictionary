Patty

The Jamaican beef (or chicken, or veggie) patty — a flaky, curry-coloured pastry sold at corner stores and bakeries across the city. Not slang exactly, but non-negotiable Toronto cultural vocabulary.

How it's used

This one's tied to Toronto's culture at large — sports, food or music that's become part of the city's identity.

“Grab me a patty and a coco bread on your way.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Jamaican-Canadian food culture. It's one of many Toronto terms that trace back to Jamaican Patois, brought into the city through Toronto's Caribbean communities. Read the fuller story in our history of Toronto slang.
