Beef & Bad Vibes

Extra

Calls someone out for being dramatic or doing way more than the situation needs. A light way to tell someone to tone it down without starting real beef.

Advertisement

How it's used

This is the vocabulary for when something, or someone, isn't hitting right.

“You're so extra, Scarbs ain't even that bad.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Broader North American slang for over-the-top behaviour, documented as active everyday Toronto vocabulary. Like a lot of GTA slang, its exact origin is debated even among people who use it every day — see our full history of Toronto slang for the bigger picture on how this vocabulary formed.
