The Six / The 6ix

Toronto's most famous nickname. Refers to the 1998 amalgamation of six municipalities — old Toronto, North York, Etobicoke, Scarborough, York and East York — into one city, and doubles as a nod to the 416 area code. Toronto rapper Jimmy Prime used it in street culture first; Drake globalized it with his 2015 album 'Views From The 6.'

How it's used

Words like this are about place — the specific streets, boundaries and nicknames that tell you exactly which part of the GTA someone means.

“You coming back to the 6 for the summer?”

Where it comes from

Origin: GTA amalgamation history, popularized by Drake. It's tied to Toronto's music and sports culture more than to a specific immigrant-language root — see our "from the block to global" section for how that side of Toronto slang spread.
