Blessed

A catch-all positive response — used as a greeting, a farewell, or a reaction to good news. When plans are locked in, they're "blessed."

Advertisement

How it's used

This is hype vocabulary — words used to big something up, whether it's an outfit, a song, or a moment.

“See you Friday then — blessed.”

Where it comes from

Origin: General affirmation, common across the GTA. Like a lot of GTA slang, its exact origin is debated even among people who use it every day — see our full history of Toronto slang for the bigger picture on how this vocabulary formed.
