Scarbs

Short for Scarborough, the large district in Toronto's east end known for its own distinct slang and hip-hop scene.

How it's used

Words like this are about place — the specific streets, boundaries and nicknames that tell you exactly which part of the GTA someone means.

“He's out in Scarbs, near Kennedy.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Shortened form of Scarborough. Like a lot of GTA slang, its exact origin is debated even among people who use it every day — see our full history of Toronto slang for the bigger picture on how this vocabulary formed.
