We The North

The Toronto Raptors' rallying slogan, unveiled in April 2014 ahead of the playoffs. It grew into a citywide identity — Canada's only NBA team representing the whole country, not just the city.

Advertisement

How it's used

This one's tied to Toronto's culture at large — sports, food or music that's become part of the city's identity.

“We The North, no explanation needed.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Toronto Raptors marketing slogan, 2014. It's tied to Toronto's music and sports culture more than to a specific immigrant-language root — see our "from the block to global" section for how that side of Toronto slang spread.
