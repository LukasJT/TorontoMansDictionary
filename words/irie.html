Hype & Approval

Irie

Means everything's cool, chill, or all-right — general good-vibes shorthand. Used the same way as "safe" in Toronto, especially in Caribbean-Canadian circles.

Advertisement

How it's used

This is hype vocabulary — words used to big something up, whether it's an outfit, a song, or a moment.

“How's it going?" "Irie, man, can't complain.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Jamaican Patois/Rastafarian term meaning "everything's good," brought into Toronto slang via the city's Jamaican diaspora. It's one of many Toronto terms that trace back to Jamaican Patois, brought into the city through Toronto's Caribbean communities. Read the fuller story in our history of Toronto slang.
