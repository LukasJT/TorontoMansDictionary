Opp Block

The block or area belonging to your opps — enemy territory.

How it's used

This is the vocabulary for when something, or someone, isn't hitting right.

“You already know not to be posted on the opp block.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Street slang, GTA rap scene. It's tied to Toronto's music and sports culture more than to a specific immigrant-language root — see our "from the block to global" section for how that side of Toronto slang spread.

Sources

This entry was compiled through general research into Toronto/GTA slang rather than one single citable source — see the full bibliography in SOURCES.md and the wider context in our history of Toronto slang.
