Hype & Approval

Bare

Means the opposite of its dictionary definition — "a lot" or "very," not "empty."

Advertisement

How it's used

This is hype vocabulary — words used to big something up, whether it's an outfit, a song, or a moment.

“There's bare people at the mall today.”

Where it comes from

Origin: UK/Caribbean-influenced GTA slang. It overlaps with UK street slang — multiethnolects like Toronto's Multicultural Toronto English and London's Multicultural London English share real linguistic DNA. More in our history page.
