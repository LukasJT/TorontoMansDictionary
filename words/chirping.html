Chirping / Chirp

Trash talk — an insult or dig thrown at someone, especially rooted in Canadian hockey culture where players "chirp" opponents. In everyday Toronto speech it just means friendly (or not-so-friendly) roasting.

How it's used

This is conversational glue — words and tags that hold a sentence together in everyday GTA speech.

“Stop chirping me, it's not that deep.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Canadian hockey/sports culture. It comes out of Canadian hockey culture rather than the city's immigrant-language history.
