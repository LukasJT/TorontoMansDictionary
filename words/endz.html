Endz

Your neighbourhood, home turf, or the area you grew up in.

How it's used

Words like this are about place — the specific streets, boundaries and nicknames that tell you exactly which part of the GTA someone means.

“Come through the endz this weekend, everyone's around.”

Where it comes from

Origin: UK/Caribbean street slang, common across the GTA. It overlaps with UK street slang — multiethnolects like Toronto's Multicultural Toronto English and London's Multicultural London English share real linguistic DNA. More in our history page.
