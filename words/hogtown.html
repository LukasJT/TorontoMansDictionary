Geography & Identity

Hogtown

A 19th-century nickname for Toronto. Originally an insult from smaller Ontario towns annoyed that Toronto politicians "hogged" the provincial legislature — later reinforced by the city's massive William Davies meatpacking plant. Toronto eventually owned the name instead of fighting it.

Where it comes from

Origin: 1890s political insult / meatpacking history. Like a lot of GTA slang, its exact origin is debated even among people who use it every day — see our full history of Toronto slang for the bigger picture on how this vocabulary formed.
