Money

Bands

Large amounts of money, typically counted in thousands (a "band" = $1,000, named after the paper band around a stack of bills).

Advertisement

How it's used

Money slang travels fast through hip-hop and drill culture, and the GTA scene has its own well-worn set of terms for it.

“Saved up bands from working all summer.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Hip-hop slang, widely used in the GTA scene. It's tied to Toronto's music and sports culture more than to a specific immigrant-language root — see our "from the block to global" section for how that side of Toronto slang spread.
