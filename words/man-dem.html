Man Dem

Originally Jamaican Patois for "those men" or "my people." In Toronto it's used loosely for your crew or group of friends, regardless of who's actually in it.

How it's used

This is relationship vocabulary — how people in the GTA refer to friends, crews, and showing respect.

“Pulled up with the man dem to the game.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Jamaican Patois: "those men / my people". It's one of many Toronto terms that trace back to Jamaican Patois, brought into the city through Toronto's Caribbean communities. Read the fuller story in our history of Toronto slang.
