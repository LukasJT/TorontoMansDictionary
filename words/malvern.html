Malvern

A neighbourhood in Scarborough with a strong cultural identity in Toronto rap and street life — often name-checked the way other cities name-check specific blocks.

How it's used

Words like this are about place — the specific streets, boundaries and nicknames that tell you exactly which part of the GTA someone means.

“That's a Malvern thing, you wouldn't get it.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Scarborough neighbourhood. It's geography slang — shorthand locals use instead of the official name. More GTA geography terms are in the dictionary's Geography & Identity category.
