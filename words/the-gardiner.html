The Gardiner

The Gardiner Expressway, the elevated highway running along Lake Ontario through downtown. Just "the Gardiner" — nobody says the full name.

Advertisement

How it's used

Words like this are about place — the specific streets, boundaries and nicknames that tell you exactly which part of the GTA someone means.

“Take the Gardiner, it'll be faster than downtown streets.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Gardiner Expressway. It's geography slang — shorthand locals use instead of the official name. More GTA geography terms are in the Geography & Identity category, or see how it maps onto real places in Toronto Slang By Neighbourhood.
