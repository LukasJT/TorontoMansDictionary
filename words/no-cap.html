No Cap

"No lie" — "cap" means a lie, so "no cap" means you're telling the truth.

How it's used

This is conversational glue — words and tags that hold a sentence together in everyday GTA speech.

“That was the best show I've been to, no cap.”

Where it comes from

Origin: AAVE, widely used across the GTA. Like a lot of GTA slang, its exact origin is debated even among people who use it every day — see our full history of Toronto slang for the bigger picture on how this vocabulary formed.
