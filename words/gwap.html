Gwap / Gwop

Money.

How it's used

Money slang travels fast through hip-hop and drill culture, and the GTA scene has its own well-worn set of terms for it.

“He's out here making gwap.”

Where it comes from

Origin: African American Vernacular English, used in GTA rap slang. It's tied to Toronto's music and sports culture more than to a specific immigrant-language root — see our "from the block to global" section for how that side of Toronto slang spread.
