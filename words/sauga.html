Sauga

Short for Mississauga, the large GTA city just west of Toronto — a way locals identify with the city without saying the whole name. Sometimes shortened even further to 'Saug.

Advertisement

How it's used

Words like this are about place — the specific streets, boundaries and nicknames that tell you exactly which part of the GTA someone means.

“Nah he's from Sauga, not the city.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Shortened form of Mississauga. It's geography slang — shorthand locals use instead of the official name. More GTA geography terms are in the Geography & Identity category, or see how it maps onto real places in Toronto Slang By Neighbourhood.
