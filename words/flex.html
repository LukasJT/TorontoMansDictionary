Flex

As a verb, to show off or stunt. Tacked onto a noun, it can also mean you're about to go do something ("gym flex" = heading to the gym).

How it's used

This is hype vocabulary — words used to big something up, whether it's an outfit, a song, or a moment.

“He's flexing that new watch on every story.”

Where it comes from

Origin: General hip-hop slang, everyday GTA usage. It's tied to Toronto's music and sports culture more than to a specific immigrant-language root — see our "from the block to global" section for how that side of Toronto slang spread.
