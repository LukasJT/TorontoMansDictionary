Long

Describes something as tedious, drawn-out, or not worth the effort — from a boring errand to a plan nobody's committed to. Saying "that's long" is a quick way to bounce on something without a whole explanation.

Advertisement

How it's used

This is the vocabulary for when something, or someone, isn't hitting right.

“Nah, walking there in this weather is long, let's just take the Rocket.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Multicultural Toronto English usage that flips the literal meaning of "long" (length) into "too much hassle". Like a lot of GTA slang, its exact origin is debated even among people who use it every day — see our full history of Toronto slang for the bigger picture on how this vocabulary formed.
