Mad

An intensifier meaning "very" or "a lot," unrelated to anger.

How it's used

This is hype vocabulary — words used to big something up, whether it's an outfit, a song, or a moment.

“That line was mad long.”

Where it comes from

Origin: General North American street slang, everyday in Toronto. Like a lot of GTA slang, its exact origin is debated even among people who use it every day — see our full history of Toronto slang for the bigger picture on how this vocabulary formed.

This entry was compiled through general research into Toronto/GTA slang rather than one single citable source — see the full bibliography in SOURCES.md and the wider context in our history of Toronto slang.
