The Beach

The local, singular short form for The Beaches neighbourhood along the Lake Ontario shoreline. Old-school residents have argued for decades over "Beach" vs. "Beaches," and dropping the s is a quiet way of signalling you're actually from there, not just visiting.

How it's used

Words like this are about place — the specific streets, boundaries and nicknames that tell you exactly which part of the GTA someone means.

“Grew up in the Beach — my parents still refuse to call it 'the Beaches.'”

Where it comes from

Origin: Local naming preference in Toronto's east-end lakeside neighbourhood, favoured over the official plural "The Beaches". It's geography slang — shorthand locals use instead of the official name.
