Cheesed / Cheesing

Angry, annoyed, or deeply irritated. To say someone is "cheesing" you means they're making you angry — the intensity ranges from mild annoyance said flatly to real anger when it's shouted.

Advertisement

How it's used

This is the vocabulary for when something, or someone, isn't hitting right.

“I'm proper cheesed right now, he cancelled last minute again.”

Where it comes from

Origin: GTA slang, exact origin unclear. Like a lot of GTA slang, its exact origin is debated even among people who use it every day — see our full history of Toronto slang for the bigger picture on how this vocabulary formed.
