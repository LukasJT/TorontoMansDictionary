Red Rocket

The older, more literal version of "the Rocket" — a direct reference to the red subway trains and streetcars.

Advertisement

How it's used

This is TTC and highway culture — the nicknames locals actually use instead of the official names.

“Caught the red rocket just in time.”

Where it comes from

Origin: TTC's original red subway cars. Like a lot of GTA slang, its exact origin is debated even among people who use it every day — see our full history of Toronto slang for the bigger picture on how this vocabulary formed.
