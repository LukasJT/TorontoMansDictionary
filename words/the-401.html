Down the 401

Highway 401, the mega-highway cutting through Toronto — famously the busiest stretch of highway in North America. "Taking the 401" is a whole personality trait.

How it's used

Words like this are about place — the specific streets, boundaries and nicknames that tell you exactly which part of the GTA someone means.

“401's a parking lot right now, take Eglinton instead.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Highway 401, busiest highway in North America. It's geography slang — shorthand locals use instead of the official name. More GTA geography terms are in the Geography & Identity category, or see how it maps onto real places in Toronto Slang By Neighbourhood.
