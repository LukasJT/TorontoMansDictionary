Wallahi

"I swear to God" in Arabic — used constantly across the GTA thanks to the city's large Somali and Arab communities, well beyond just those speaking Arabic at home.

Advertisement

How it's used

This is conversational glue — words and tags that hold a sentence together in everyday GTA speech.

“Wallahi I'm not lying, ask anyone who was there.”

Where it comes from

Origin: Arabic, common via Toronto's Somali and Arab communities. It's part of the Somali- and Arabic-rooted vocabulary that entered Toronto slang through the city's East African and Middle Eastern communities. Read more in our history of Toronto slang.
